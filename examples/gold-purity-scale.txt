ပဲရည် (Karat)
------------

24 ပဲရည် = 100% pure gold = 24K
1 ပဲရည် = 4.1667% gold
16 ပဲရည် = 66.67% pure gold = 16K gold
20 ပဲရည် = 83.33% pure gold = 20K gold
22 ပဲရည် = 91.67% pure gold = 22K gold

Formula:
ပဲရည် ÷ 24 × 100 = gold purity %
ပဲရည် = karat × 1

16 ပဲရည် = 16K gold = 66.67% gold + 33.33% other metals
